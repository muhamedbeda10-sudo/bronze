# Quality Control

**Pending Inspection:** orders sitting at a quality stage, urgent first, with how long they have waited.
Decisions (full quality access): Pass Stage (on to the next stage, or complete into FG stock), Reject to Last (back one stage as rework), Reject to Start (back to the first stage — re-cast).
Each decision records the quantity inspected and rejected; any rejected piece needs its rejection reason, which feeds the scrap analysis and the dashboard.
**Inspection History:** every decision with WO, stage, result, material, quantities, reason and inspector — traceability for customer complaints.
