# Overtime

**Requested Overtime → New Request:** date, workshop, operator, time from–to (the hours follow, past midnight allowed, 12 at most), reason. One request per operator and day. A supervisor requests for their own workshop.
Approval by planning or overtime full access, never by the person who asked; a rejection needs its reason. Planning sees the same list in Planning Notification.
**Overtime History:** approved and rejected requests with who processed them, filtered by period and workshop, the approved total, Export and Print for payroll.
Approved hours feed the dashboard's Overtime Hours.
